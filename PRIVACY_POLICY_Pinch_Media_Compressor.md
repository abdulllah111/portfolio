# Privacy Policy for Pinch

**Last Updated:** September 21, 2026

Pinch ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed by **Nasimjon** as a commercial photo and video compression utility for iOS and iPadOS. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your personal media. 

This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data privacy when you use Pinch.

---

### 1. Summary — Zero Data Collection
**Pinch does NOT collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or media files.**  
All photo and video compression happens **100% locally and offline on your device**. We do not operate remote servers, user databases, or cloud processing systems.

---

### 2. Access to Photos and Videos
To provide its core functionality, Pinch requests permission to access your device's Photo Library (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` and `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`).

* **Strictly Local On-Device Processing:** The App accesses selected photos and videos solely to compress them and save the optimized copies back to your Photos library.
* **Zero Remote Uploads:** Your media files (photos, videos, timestamps, and EXIF metadata) **never leave your iPhone or iPad**. No files are ever sent over the network or uploaded to external servers.
* **Safe Cleanup:** When you choose to delete original files after compression, Pinch uses the standard Apple iOS Photos API to move them to your system "Recently Deleted" album, where they are kept safely for 30 days and can be restored at any time.

---

### 3. Information We Do NOT Collect
* **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, IP addresses, or hardware identifiers.
* **No Analytics or Trackers:** Pinch does not contain third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK, or Mixpanel).
* **No Advertising:** Pinch does not serve third-party ads and does not access the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
* **No Cross-App Tracking:** The App does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

---

### 4. Local Preferences Storage
Pinch stores basic application preferences (such as selected language and compression quality presets) locally on your device using Apple's standard `UserDefaults`. This configuration data remains strictly on your device and is never synchronized with external servers.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services
Pinch does not integrate with or send data to any third-party APIs, remote processors, or external data brokers.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
Because Pinch does not collect any personal data from any user, the App does not knowingly collect or solicit any information from children.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected in this document with an updated "Last Updated" date.
